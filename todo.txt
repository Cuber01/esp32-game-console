Driver info:
- we use 4-line SPI (hardwired by producer)

4-line SPI pins used:
CSX (CS): Chip selection signal
WRX (SCL): Clock
DCX (DC): Data is regarded as a command when WRX is low. Data is regarded as a parameter or data when WRX is high
SDA (SDA): Serial input/output data
